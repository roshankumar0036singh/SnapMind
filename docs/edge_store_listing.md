# SnapMind — AI Research & Knowledge Assistant 🧠✨

**Transform your browser into a centralized knowledge powerhouse.** SnapMind is an advanced AI research copilot that brings Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology directly into your sidebar. Designed for researchers, developers, and power users, it helps you organize, analyze, and connect information across the web.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

### 🔍 Real-Time Web Intelligence
Stop skim-reading and start deep-diving. SnapMind analyzes the active tab in real-time, allowing you to summarize complex articles, extract specific data points, and ask deep questions about any webpage instantly.

### 📚 Seamless Document Ingestion
Consolidate your knowledge in one place. Ingest PDFs, Markdown files, and technical documentation directly into your research library. SnapMind indexes your data securely, making it immediately searchable and ready for AI-powered interaction.

### 🕸️ Interactive Knowledge Graphs
Visualize the "hidden" connections. SnapMind automatically maps relationships between your documents, tags, and research sessions. Explore your personal knowledge base through an interactive 3D graph to discover non-obvious insights.

### 📍 Cited & Verifiable Answers
Trust your results. Every response from SnapMind is backed by direct citations from your source material. Click any citation to jump directly to the exact paragraph or page in your library.

### 🤖 Autonomous Research Agents
Deploy specialized AI agents that can browse the live web, synthesize data from multiple sources, and generate comprehensive research reports—all while you focus on high-level decision making.
