Extension Powers

One-Click Ingestion

Reading documentation? Click ingest. The extension automatically scrapes the page, cleans the HTML, and embeds it into your active local DB.

Contextual Querying

Highlight any text on the web, right-click, and ask SnapMind to explain or refactor it using your existing local codebase context.

Local API Bridge

The extension communicates exclusively with your Desktop's Python backend (http://localhost:8000). Your browsing data never leaves your network.
